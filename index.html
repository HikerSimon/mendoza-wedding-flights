Your trip

    Routes worth checking

    There are no nonstop flights from North America to Mendoza. Everyone connects through one of these gateways.

    Good to know

    Before you book

    Seeing no flights yet?

    Airlines release seats about 11 months ahead. Late October 2027 dates start appearing from December 2026, and the smaller South American carriers load later than that.

    Flights south leave at night

    Most long-haul flights depart in the evening and land the next morning. The plan above already has you leaving home the day before you arrive.

    Book the hop on one ticket if you can

    If the Santiago or Buenos Aires connection is on the same booking as your long flight, the airline rebooks you when something runs late. Separate tickets need a buffer of several hours, or a night.

    Changing airports in Buenos Aires

    If you connect to Mendoza the same day, look for a flight leaving from Ezeiza. Switching to Aeroparque means a cross-town transfer and at least four hours between flights.

    The Andes bus

    Santiago to Mendoza by road takes 6 to 8 hours including the border stop. Snow can close the pass in winter. By late October it is normally open.

    Passports

    US and Canadian passport holders can visit Argentina and Chile as tourists without a visa. Check your passport has six months left, and confirm the rules for your own nationality.

    Things To Do

    You're coming a long way, so make a trip of it. Here's what we love in Mendoza, and where to spend extra days on either side of the wedding.

    While you're there

    Things to do in Mendoza

    Book winery visits and lunches ahead; almost none take walk-ins. Shops close for siesta from about 1 to 5, and dinner starts at 9 or later.

    Luján de Cuyo wineries

    Half day · 30 minutes from town

    The home of Malbec and the easiest tasting day. Catena Zapata, Lagarde and Ruca Malen are well-known stops; plan two visits and a long lunch at one of them.

    Uco Valley

    Full day · 90 minutes south

    Higher, newer vineyards right under the snow line. Zuccardi Valle de Uco and Salentein are the big names. Hire a driver so nobody has to skip the pours.

    Bike the Maipú wine road

    Half day · 25 minutes from town

    Rent bikes and ride between small family wineries and olive oil producers. Relaxed, cheap and good for a group.

    The high Andes on Ruta 7

    Full day · about 3 hours each way

    Drive or take a tour up through Uspallata to Puente del Inca and the Aconcagua viewpoint, the highest peak outside Asia. Bring a warm layer; it is cold up there even in spring.

    Potrerillos

    Half or full day · 1 hour west

    A turquoise reservoir in the foothills with rafting on the Mendoza River, horseback rides and short hikes.

    Cacheuta hot springs

    Half day · 1 hour west

    Thermal pools in a river canyon. A good slow day after the wedding, and easy to pair with Potrerillos.

    Parque San Martín and the plazas

    A few hours · in town

    Walk the big park up to Cerro de la Gloria for the view over the city, then wander Plaza Independencia and the four smaller plazas around it.

    Eat and drink in town

    Evenings

    Order an asado and Mendoza-style empanadas. Arístides Villanueva street is the bar and restaurant strip. For a splurge, book 1884 by Francis Mallmann or Azafrán.

    Four stops

    Where to spend the extra days

    Mendoza MDZ

    Wine country at the foot of the Andes. Late October is spring: warm days around 75°F (24°C), cool nights.

    • Give yourself two days before the wedding. Jet lag is mild (1–4 hours from North America) but the journey is long.
    • Malbec tastings in Luján de Cuyo and the Uco Valley book up, so reserve ahead.
    • The airport is 20 minutes from downtown.

    Santiago SCL

    The biggest long-haul gateway near Mendoza, and often the cheapest way in.

    • 2 nights covers the city. Add one for Valparaíso or the Casablanca wine valley.
    • Fly to Mendoza in 55 minutes, or take the 7-hour bus over the Los Libertadores pass, one of the great road trips on the continent.
    • Chile is strict about food at customs. Declare fruit, nuts and snacks.

    Bariloche BRC · Patagonia

    The quickest way into Patagonia if you don't have the week it takes to go farther south to El Calafate or Ushuaia.

    • 3 to 4 nights. Lakes, forest and snow-capped peaks in Nahuel Huapi National Park, with chocolate shops and craft beer in town.
    • Rent a car for the Ruta de los Siete Lagos: Ruta 40 from Villa La Angostura to San Martín de los Andes, 107 km past seven lakes. It works as a long day from Bariloche and is better with a night in San Martín.
    • About 2 hours by air from Mendoza or Buenos Aires. Nonstops from Mendoza don't run every day, so check before you fix dates.
    • Early November is spring: cool, quiet, snow still on the peaks. Pack layers.

    Buenos Aires AEP / EZE

    Steak, tango, bookshops and late nights. Best saved for after the wedding.

    • 3 nights is a good minimum. Stay in Palermo or Recoleta.
    • Two airports: flights from Mendoza mostly land at Aeroparque (AEP), in town. Flights home leave from Ezeiza (EZE), about an hour out.
    • Jacaranda trees bloom purple in early November.
